The Opponent

Creatures and hostile NPCs use the Opponent sheet. The Opponents compendium holds the creatures of the core book, ready to use.

The header

  • HD: the Opponent's hit dice;
  • HP: current and maximum hit points;
  • AP: armour points, deducted automatically from the damage it takes;
  • Penalty: the malus suffered by the characters who attack or resist it.

On an unlocked sheet, changing the HD recomputes the maximum HP (4 per HD, keeping the wounds already taken) and the penalty (−1 per HD beyond the first).

The tabs

  • Description: the creature's text (open by default).
  • Attacks, Abilities, Magic: three lists, each with a + button when the sheet is unlocked.

Only attacks, opponent abilities and spells can be dropped on this sheet. Right-click an element to edit or delete it.

Attacks

An attack is defined by a damage die, fixed damage and a number of attacks. When it has a damage die, a die icon rolls its damage (die + fixed damage, for example "d8 + 2"). If a character is targeted, the chat card offers to apply the damage to them.

An attack with fixed damage only has no roll icon: its damage applies when you pick it for a counter-attack (see @@page:jets@@).

Abilities and spells

  • Opponent abilities track limited uses like the characters' abilities: right-click to Use, Increase or Reset.
  • Spells are cast with the die icon (sanity check); the spell's die goes down on a failure.
