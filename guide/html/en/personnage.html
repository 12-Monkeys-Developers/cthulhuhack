The Character

Locking the sheet

Every sheet opens locked: you can play (roll dice, use abilities) without changing a value by mistake. The switch at the top left of the title bar unlocks it. The sheet then widens and lets you:

  • enter the name, occupation, skills, archetype and a short description;
  • edit the saves, the resources (value and maximum), the hit points and the damage dice;
  • create items, weapons, abilities and spells with the + buttons;
  • drop elements from the sidebar or a compendium: a locked sheet refuses drops.

Click the portrait to change it when the sheet is unlocked (right-click to go back to the default image). On a locked sheet, hover over it to show it to the players. Right-clicking the character's name searches the world for that name.

The header: resources, health and damage

  • Flashlights, Smokes, Sanity, plus Wealth and the miscellaneous resource depending on the settings: click the name to roll the die. On a failure the die automatically goes down one step. A die below its maximum is highlighted.
  • Hit Points and Hit Dice, depending on the "Health Display Option" setting.
  • Armed damage and Unarmed damage: click to roll damage. If an Opponent is targeted, its armour is deducted on the card.

The left column

  • The six saves: click a name to roll it (see @@page:jets@@).
  • Fortune and the Adrenaline tokens, when these options are on (see @@page:options@@).
  • The Conditions the character suffers (see below).

The tabs

  • Description: the biography.
  • Equipment (open by default): weapons, then items, encumbrance when the option is on, and a free text area for the rest of the gear.
  • Abilities & Magic: special abilities, then spells and rituals.
  • Notes: a free text area.

In the lists, clicking a line unfolds its description; drag a line onto another one to reorder the list.

Weapons and items

Each weapon or item can have a material die: the die icon rolls a material check, and the die goes down one step on a failure. Weapons also show their range.

To attack with a weapon, drag it to the macro bar (see @@page:outils@@), or target an Opponent and roll a Strength or Dexterity save while declaring the attack (see @@page:jets@@).

On an unlocked sheet, the + button of the Equipment tab creates a weapon; with Shift + click it creates an item. The new element's sheet opens locked: flip its switch to fill it in.

The right-click menu

Right-clicking a weapon, item, ability, spell or condition opens a menu:

  • Send to chat for all: posts the description in the chat (when the element has one);
  • Send to chat for GM: offered to players, to pass you a description privately;
  • Edit: opens the element's sheet;
  • Delete: removes the element from the character.

Special abilities

An ability can have a limited number of uses with a period: permanent, per scenario, per session, per day, per night, per hour, per scene, per fight or per moment. The list shows the remaining uses and the date of the last use. The right-click menu also offers:

  • Use: removes one use and records the date;
  • Increase: gives one use back;
  • Reset: appears when no use is left, and restores all uses.

The standard abilities of the compendium are recognised by the system: once dropped on the sheet, they are offered automatically as an advantage in the roll dialog when they apply. An ability that is not "Multiple" cannot be added twice.

For a personal ability, tick Custom and Advantage given on its sheet, then describe the advantage: it will be offered in the roll dialog.

Drop standard abilities from the compendium rather than recreating them with the + button: only dropped abilities are recognised by the roll dialog.

Spells and rituals

Each spell shows its type (spell or ritual), its sanity die and its incantation. The die icon rolls a sanity check; on a failure the spell's die goes down one step.

Creating a character: archetypes and example characters

The Example characters compendium holds a ready-to-play investigator per archetype: drag one into the actors tab to import it.

To start from a blank sheet, unlock it and drop an archetype on it. It fills in the archetype name, Flashlights, Smokes and Sanity (value and maximum), the Hit Dice, Wealth and the armed and unarmed damage dice. Hit points, saves and abilities are left for you to enter.

Conditions: Out of Action, Temporary Insanity and Shock

When a character suffers a consequence, unlock their sheet and drop the condition from the Out of action, Temporary insanity or Shock compendium. It appears under "Conditions" in the left column (hover over it to read its description) and an icon is shown on the token. Dropping a condition the character already has does nothing.

Some conditions are applied automatically:

  • Mild Concussion, Staggered and Winded: a disadvantage on save rolls;
  • Cracked Bones: −4 to the Strength, Dexterity and Constitution saves.

The others are reminders: apply their effects at the table. To remove a condition, right-click it and choose Delete.
