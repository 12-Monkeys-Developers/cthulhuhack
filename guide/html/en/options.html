System settings

The settings are in Game Settings → Configure Settings → Cthulhu Hack. They apply to everyone and only the GM can change them. Some of them require a page reload, which Foundry offers when you save.

Cthulhu Hack Reissue

Turns on the rules of the reissue (on by default). In Foundry this means:

  • the character's skills are offered as a modifier in the roll dialog;
  • with the Adrenaline option, the character's two tokens are shown as Fortune tokens in the players manager;
  • the shared Fortune pool is no longer shown in the players manager.

Adrenaline Option

Adds two tokens below the saves of each character. Clicking a token passes it from the player to the GM, and back. The tokens are also shown in the players manager.

Fortune Option and Fortune available

Turns on a pool of Fortune tokens shared by the whole table. The number of tokens left is shown on every character sheet and updates live. You set it here, or directly from a sheet (see @@page:outils@@).

Wealth

  • No wealth management: wealth does not appear on the sheet.
  • Wealth as a fixed dice value: wealth is a die shown on the sheet, which you manage by hand.
  • Wealth is managed as a resource: wealth is rolled and goes down on a failure, like Flashlights or Smokes.

Miscellaneous Resource

Enter a name (for example "Respect") to add an extra resource to every sheet. It is rolled like the other resources. Leave it empty to disable it.

Health Display Option

  • Hit points: the sheet shows HP; the "Apply" button of damage cards removes them automatically.
  • Hit dice: the sheet shows the Hit Dice. When a character is hurt, the damage card offers a Hit Dice roll instead of removing HP.
  • Hit points and hit dice (default): both are shown; damage is removed from HP.

Display Opponent malus

When a player targets an Opponent before rolling:

  • on (default): the Opponent's malus is filled in automatically in the roll dialog and lowers the threshold;
  • off: players do not see the malus. Only you see it, in the roll dialog and on the chat card ("Opponent's malus (hidden)" line). It is not removed from the threshold: take it into account yourself when you announce the result.

Simultaneous damage

When a character succeeds an attack against a targeted Opponent, their damage is rolled at once, on the same card. When off, the player rolls it with the card's button. In both cases a failure hands over to the GM for the counter-attack (see @@page:jets@@).

Use the encumbrance option

Adds encumbrance to weapons and items: a size when equipped, a size when not equipped, and a status Equipped, Unequipped or Home. The sheet's Equipment tab shows the current encumbrance, computed automatically, against the maximum you enter. Items left at home do not count.

Customising the look

The system's colours are defined with CSS variables: a module (a campaign module, for example) can redefine them to restyle the sheets and the chat cards.
