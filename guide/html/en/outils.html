GM tools

The players manager

The Players status window opens automatically for the GM when the world loads. If you close it, reopen it with the Players Manager button of the Cthulhu Hack sidebar tab.

It shows one line per connected player and their character: resources, hit dice and hit points depending on the settings, Adrenaline tokens (or Fortune tokens with the reissue) and the six saves. It updates live during the game.

  • Click a column header to ask all players for that roll: a "Roll of … for all !" message is posted.
  • Click a cell to ask that player only: they get a private message, with a drum roll.
  • Click the character's name to open their sheet; hover over it to see their abilities and spells.

The player answers by clicking Let's roll ! in the message: their character's roll dialog opens.

Fortune

With the Fortune option, every character sheet shows "Fortune : N", the number of tokens left for the whole table. Right-click it (GM only):

  • Use: removes one token and announces in the chat who spent it and how many are left;
  • Increase: adds one token to the pool.

Without the reissue rules, the total is also shown at the top of the players manager.

Search

The Search button of the Cthulhu Hack tab opens a window where you type a text. The system searches the world's journals, actors and items (not the compendiums) and sends you, and only you, the list of results as links. Above 20 results it asks you to refine your search.

The Highlight On / Off button of the message highlights the searched text in open journals. Shortcut: right-clicking the name of a character or an Opponent, on its sheet, searches for that name.

Sharing a description

On a character sheet, right-clicking a weapon, item, ability, spell or condition lets you send its description to the chat for the whole table. Players can also send it to the GM only.

The macro bar

Drag an element to the macro bar to create a shortcut:

Dragged elementWhat the macro does
An actor or a journalOpens its sheet or the journal
A save, a resource or damage from the sheetRolls it for that character
A character's weaponAttack roll (Strength in melee, Dexterity at range); Shift + click: material roll
A character's itemMaterial roll
An ability, of a character or an OpponentUses it (removes one use)
A spell, of a character or an OpponentSanity roll
An Opponent's attackDamage roll

Weapon, item, ability, spell and attack macros act on the selected token (failing that, the weapon macro uses the player's assigned character): one macro therefore works for several characters who own the element. Save, resource and damage macros stay tied to the character they came from. Drag elements from a sheet: an item from the sidebar or a compendium does not create a macro.

Consequence tables

The Tables compendium holds the Out of Action, Temporary Insanity and Shock tables. The Macros compendium offers a draw on each one, visible to the GM only. Import the tables into your world first: the macros find them by name.
