Rolls and messages

Available rolls

  • Save: click a save name on the sheet. Success if the d20 is lower than or equal to the threshold.
  • Resource: click Flashlights, Smokes, Sanity, Wealth or the miscellaneous resource. On a failure the die goes down one step.
  • Material: die icon of a weapon or item. On a failure the material die goes down.
  • Sanity: die icon of a spell.
  • Damage: click a character's armed or unarmed damage, or the die icon of an Opponent's attack.
  • Attack: a weapon macro, or a Strength or Dexterity save declared as an attack against a targeted Opponent.

The roll dialog

Every roll opens a dialog gathering everything that changes the result:

  • Target: if the player targeted a token (T key or right-click on the token), its image, name and opponent malus are shown. For a damage roll the GM also sees its armour. If several tokens are targeted, only the first one counts and a warning says so.
  • Attack: when an Opponent is targeted, the player declares an Armed or Unarmed attack (with the matching damage die). For a Strength or Dexterity save, a No attack choice is offered, and selected by default.
  • Save and Malus: the save value and a malus from 0 to −10, pre-filled with the targeted Opponent's malus.
  • Final threshold: the threshold to beat, updated on every change.
  • Modifiers: the occupation, the skills (reissue rules), the standard abilities that apply and the personal abilities that give an advantage. Clicking a modifier adds one step of advantage.
  • Advantages and disadvantages: a five-step slider, from double disadvantage to double advantage.
  • Roll visibility: public, private to the GM, blind or self only.

Conditions that impose a disadvantage (Mild Concussion, Staggered, Winded) are already taken into account when the dialog opens. With an advantage the system rolls extra dice and keeps the best one; with a disadvantage it keeps the worst one.

Chat cards

Each roll produces a card that sums up:

  • the character (portrait and name at the top of the card) and their target when there is one ("against Ghoul");
  • the declared attack type (Armed or Unarmed);
  • the dice rolled: with an advantage or a disadvantage, the discarded die is greyed out;
  • the result against the threshold, the formula and the modifiers used;
  • the threshold breakdown, which can be unfolded (save, opponent malus, malus);
  • the Success or Failure banner;
  • for a lost resource, how the die changed (for example "d4 → 0").

Clicking a token portrait in the card pans the scene to that token.

Combat from the chat

When a character attacks a targeted Opponent, the whole exchange fits on a single card, updated as the actions go.

The attack succeeds

  1. The card reads "Hit! Waiting for …'s damage". The player (or the GM) clicks Roll armed damage or Roll unarmed damage. With the Simultaneous damage setting, this roll is made automatically.
  2. The damage is added to the card. Only you see the armour deducted and the real damage.
  3. The Apply … damage to … button, for the GM only, removes the real damage from the Opponent's HP. The card then shows how the HP changed.

The attack fails: the counter-attack

  1. The card says the Opponent strikes back and waits for the GM. Click Choose the attack.
  2. A dialog lists the Opponent's attacks, with their dice or fixed damage. Pick one and click Roll damage.
  3. The damage is added to the card, with the Apply … damage to … button that removes it from the character's HP.

If health is tracked with Hit dice only, there are no HP to remove: a failure directly offers the character a Hit Dice roll, and the Apply button is replaced by that roll for a wounded character.

Other damage rolls

  • A character's damage roll against a targeted Opponent deducts its armour and offers the Apply button.
  • An Opponent's attack rolled from its sheet against a targeted character also offers the Apply button.

Asking for a roll from a journal: @jet

In a journal, or in an actor's or item's description, you can insert a roll request link. When you click it, a "Roll of … for all !" message is posted in the chat; each player clicks Let's roll ! to roll with the character assigned to them (in the user configuration).

Syntax

@jet[target]{}(advantage)

The braces and parentheses are required, even when empty. @roll works the same way as @jet.

TargetRequested roll
str, dex, con, wis, int, chaSave
flashlights, smokes, sanFlashlights, Smokes, Sanity
wealthDice, hitDice, miscellaneousWealth, Hit Dice, miscellaneous resource

The advantage is optional: ++ double advantage, + advantage, - disadvantage, -- double disadvantage. It is carried over to the player's roll dialog.

Examples:

  • @jet[wis]{}(-): Wisdom save with disadvantage;
  • @jet[flashlights]{}(): Flashlights roll.
